## Accessibility Compliance Note

The demonstration targets WCAG 2.1 Level AA and includes semantic structure, keyboard-operable controls, visible focus, reduced-motion support, high contrast, text resizing, accessible error summaries, status announcements, non-colour-only information and an operational low-bandwidth mode. WCAG 2.2 and Level AAA improvements are applied where feasible. Formal production compliance remains subject to the limitations above.
